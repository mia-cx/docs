past of mine is future yours  
cant seem to remember  
when i would become you

---

what has been will come  
what has come will be  
respite of clouded memory  
we may not ever see

---

take a breath and rest your eyes  
think but pay no mind  
there is so much yet to understand  
look not but listen

---

it felt like i was losing myself  
my mind wandered  
as the river slowly adopted the stars

the boundary between reflections  
losing definition  
and opposites became equal

something near but so distant  
familiar but so estranged

when it suddenly stared back  
locking me back into place  
seeming to finally comprehend

---

i sat up and i was severed  
my carnal bearings shed  
these somatic bounds no more  
pulling at my ego-thread  
the fabric of my self unraveled  
left as nothing but alterity
